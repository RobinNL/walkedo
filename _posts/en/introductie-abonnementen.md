The last newsletter announced that from 1 January I will be moving to subscriptions.

So what does that mean for you?

No more strip cards. No more hanging them up, putting them down, or keeping track of them.

No more payment requests. An inbox full of them? From Walkedo you will now only get the occasional newsletter.

No more confusion about days off. From January, six weeks per year are already deducted from every client's monthly amount. **

This means no new cards will be written from 2025 onwards.

What I would ask of you is to set up a standing order from 1 January to account number NL04 KNAB 0617 6989 37. Below you can pick the amount that applies to you.

| Walks       | Amount             |
|-------------|--------------------|
| 1x per week | 51.75 per month    |
| 2x per week | 103.50 per month   |
| 3x per week | 155.25 per month   |
| 4x per week | 207.00 per month   |
| 5x per week | 257.75 per month   |


**
Here is how the six-week deduction works. A single walk is charged at 13.50. On that basis the cheapest subscription would come to 13.50 × 52 weeks = 702 euros per year. Divided by 12 months that is 58.50. Because everyone is entitled to days off, I charge for six weeks fewer: 13.50 × 46 weeks / 12 months = 51.75.
