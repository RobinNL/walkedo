By popular request, an overview of the days off Walkedo observes. Your subscription is lower because these days are already accounted for. In 2026 these are the days concerned:

- Thursday 12 February, training day
- Thursday 26 February, training day
- Thursday 19 March, training day
- Monday 6 April, Easter Monday
- Thursday 9 April, training day
- Thursday 23 April, theory exam
- Monday 27 April, King's Day
- Tuesday 5 May, Liberation Day
- Wednesday 13 May, theory exam resit (to be confirmed)
- Monday 25 May, Whit Monday
- Friday 5 June, practical exam (to be confirmed)
- Monday 20 July to 9 August, summer holiday
- Monday 21 December to 1 January 2027
