By popular request, an overview of the days off Walkedo observes. Your subscription is lower because these days are already accounted for. In 2025 these are the days concerned:

- New Year's Day: Wednesday 1 January
- Good Friday: Friday 18 April
- Easter (Sunday and Monday): Sunday 20 April and Monday 21 April
- Liberation Day: Monday 5 May
- Ascension Day: Thursday 29 May
- Whit Monday: Monday 9 June
- Summer holiday: 7 July to 28 July
- Christmas holiday: 21 December to Thursday 2 January
